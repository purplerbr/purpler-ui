New `FloatingContactLink` atom (`@purplerbr/ui/floating-contact-link`): a contact link fixed to the bottom-right corner, clear of the iOS safe areas, in the brand colors. Icon only with a 48 px target on phones, icon and text from `md`. It always opens in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and reads "(abre em nova aba)" after the label. It sits below the Sheet overlay and the skip link, and works without JavaScript. The destination, the icon and the label come from props.
