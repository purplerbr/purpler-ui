New `MarketingHeader` organism (`@purplerbr/ui/marketing-header`) for the Purpler site: skip link to `#conteudo`, logo, nav links with `aria-current`, a CTA and a mobile menu in a `Sheet`. Without JavaScript the menu button hides and the links show under the bar, so prerendered pages stay navigable. Links, CTA and labels come from props.
