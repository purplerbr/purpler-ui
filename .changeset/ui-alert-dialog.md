Add `AlertDialog` (`@purplerbr/ui/alert-dialog`): a confirmation on Radix AlertDialog that traps the focus, starts on cancel, closes on Esc, ignores clicks outside and returns the focus to the trigger. `AlertDialogAction` and `AlertDialogCancel` take the `Button` `variant` and `size` (use `destructive` for actions that cannot be undone).
